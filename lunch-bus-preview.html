How we cook

Cooked on campus, not reheated

Nothing is made the day before. We pull onto campus before lunch and cook in the trailer, so meals go out within minutes of coming off the heat rather than sitting in a warmer for hours.

The early price is the real price

Pre-order before the monthly deadline and every meal is eight dollars. Order after it and a meal is ten. The difference covers a second shopping trip and a smaller prep run, and it is the only pricing rule we have. There is no membership, no minimum and no charge for leaving something off a plate.

Kid-first, not kid-only

Every meal is built around something a child will recognise and eat, with a vegetable and fresh fruit alongside rather than hidden inside it. Portions suit a younger student, and a double portion is available for older ones.

Straight answers about allergens

Each meal is labelled with the allergens it contains. The trailer is a shared kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, soy and egg, so we do not claim any meal is free of cross contact. If your child has a severe allergy, email before you order and we will tell you honestly whether we can serve them safely.

Licensing and safety

List your El Dorado County health permit number, your food handler or manager certification, and your liability insurance carrier. Put the permit number in plain text where a parent can see it. This is the single strongest trust signal on a food website and most new vendors leave it off.

Our part in the school

Lunch Bus operates with the knowledge of the school office and serves on the campus during the regular lunch period. We are a family business, not a school program, and the school does not handle payment or refunds. Anything about an order comes to us directly.

Questions about ordering

Everything parents ask before their first order. If yours is not here, email and we will answer it the same school day.

What does a meal cost?

Eight dollars if you pre-order before the monthly deadline, and ten dollars after it. That covers the main dish and everything served alongside it. A double portion is two dollars more either way, and milk and juice boxes are two dollars each.

When do I need to order by?

The pre-order deadline for each month is shown at the top of the ordering page. Order by then and every meal is eight dollars.

Ordering stays open after that at ten dollars a meal, up until two days before each meal is served. Once a meal is inside that two-day window it disappears from the calendar and cannot be added.

Why does waiting cost more?

Pre-orders tell us what to buy. A late order means a second shopping trip and a smaller, less predictable prep run, and the price reflects that. Nothing about the food changes, and a late order is served exactly like an early one.

I ordered early. Does a later addition change my first price?

No. Each meal is priced when you order it. Meals bought before the deadline stay at eight dollars whatever you add afterwards.

My child has a food allergy.

Enter it when you add your student and it prints on our kitchen list beside every meal they ordered. Common allergens are labelled on each menu item.

The trailer is a single small kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, soy and egg in a shared space. We cannot guarantee any meal is free of cross contact, and we would rather say so than reassure you and be wrong. If the allergy is severe, email before you order and we will give you a straight answer about whether we can serve your child safely.

Can I order for more than one child?

Yes. Add each student, then switch between them as you pick days. The receipt separates the order by child.

Some meals have two versions. How does that work?

On pizza and sandwich days you choose the version when you add the meal. Both cost the same. Your choice goes on the kitchen list, so your student gets what you picked.

What happens if my child is absent?

Email before school starts that morning and we credit the meal toward your next order. Once the trailer is loaded the food is already made.

Can I get a refund?

In full, any time before the pre-order deadline. After that the ingredients are bought, so we issue a credit toward your next order instead.

Where does the trailer park, and how does my child get their food?

We set up in the lot by the parish hall and cook on site. Orders then go out grade by grade, each one labelled with your child's name, so nobody has to work out what they ordered or wait in a line for long.

What if my student forgets they ordered?

It does not matter. Every order is labelled with a name and goes out with that child's grade, so there is nothing for your student to remember. If a meal goes unclaimed we hold it through the lunch period and check with the office.

Is this run by the school?

No. Lunch Bus is a family business serving on campus. The school does not process payments or refunds, so bring any question about an order to us.

Do you serve every week of the month?

We serve the dates listed on the menu and nothing else. Holidays, half days and school breaks are already excluded, so what you see on the calendar is what we run.

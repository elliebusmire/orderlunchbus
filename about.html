How we cook

How the food is prepared

We pull onto campus before lunch and cook in the trailer, so meals go out within minutes of coming off the heat. Nothing is made the day before and reheated.

Pricing

Pre-order before the monthly deadline and every meal is eight dollars. Order after it and a meal is ten, which covers a second shopping trip and a smaller prep run. There is no membership, no minimum, and no charge for leaving something off a plate.

What is in a meal

Every meal is built around something a child will recognise, with a vegetable and fresh fruit alongside it. Portions suit a younger student, and a double portion is available for older ones.

Allergens

Each meal is labelled with the allergens it contains. The trailer is a shared kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, soy and egg, so we do not claim any meal is free of cross contact. If your child has a severe allergy, email before you order and we will tell you honestly whether we can serve them safely.

Licensing and safety

List your El Dorado County health permit number, your food handler or manager certification, and your liability insurance carrier. Put the permit number in plain text where a parent can see it. This is the single strongest trust signal on a food website and most new vendors leave it off.

Our part in the school

Lunch Bus operates with the knowledge of the school office and serves on campus during the regular lunch period. It is a family business rather than a school program, so the school does not handle payment or refunds. Anything about an order comes to us directly.
